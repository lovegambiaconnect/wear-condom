Consent must be voluntary, specific and revocable for future participation; coercion, threats, intoxication preventing consent, exploitation or trafficking are prohibited.
  • Absolutely prohibited: any sexual or intimate content involving a minor or a person who appears to be a minor; sexualized age-play; non-consensual intimate imagery; hidden-camera or voyeuristic material; revenge porn; coercion; trafficking; incest; bestiality; sexual violence; or content obtained unlawfully.

We may review reports, remove or restrict content, disable accounts, preserve relevant records, and cooperate with competent authorities where appropriate or legally required. Review and moderation are not a guarantee that all content is lawful or that every violation will be detected. Do not download, copy, screenshot or share intimate content without permission.

If you believe you are depicted in content without consent, report it promptly with the content link and a safe way to contact you. We will assess the report and take appropriate action, which may include temporary restriction while reviewing.
