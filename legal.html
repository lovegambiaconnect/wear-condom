COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Terms, privacy & safety.

WEAR CONDOM is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. This page is a starter policy and should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch.

18+ access

You must be at least 18 years old to register or use this service. The on-site age prompt is a self-attestation and does not replace robust age assurance where required by law.

Consent and lawful content

Only upload or share content you own or are authorised to share. Every identifiable person must be an adult and must have freely consented to the creation and sharing of the content. Non-consensual intimate imagery, coercion, exploitation, trafficking, impersonation and any content involving minors are prohibited.

Privacy and safety

Do not share another person's private information, threaten, harass, stalk or pressure members. Do not ask anyone for intimate images. Use reporting tools and contact support to flag concerns. Public creator profiles are subject to moderation.

Payments

Creator tier prices are currently informational only. WEAR CONDOM does not currently process subscriptions or payouts. Do not send money to another member in reliance on a displayed tier.

Moderation

Accounts and submissions may be restricted, removed or suspended for policy violations. Moderation review is not a guarantee that content is lawful or safe.

Contact

Contact the platform operator through the support contact published on the official website once configured.
