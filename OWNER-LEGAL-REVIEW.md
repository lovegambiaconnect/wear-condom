# OWNER-ONLY LEGAL REVIEW CHECKLIST — WEAR CONDOM

**Private internal reminder — not for visitors.**

The public policy page contains practical policy templates, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. Before launching broadly or accepting uploads/payments, have qualified counsel review the laws that apply in every country where the platform operates.

Review at minimum:
- Adult-only access and age assurance requirements.
- Consent, performer identity/age documentation, and recordkeeping.
- Privacy notice accuracy, cookies, analytics, ad partners, data retention, and user rights.
- Content moderation, reporting, illegal-content response, and law-enforcement requests.
- Copyright/takedown procedures and repeat-infringer handling.
- Consumer disclosures, subscriptions, payment/refund rules, and advertising.
- Hosting/provider rules and applicable cross-border obligations.

Keep this checklist in the private repository; do not publish it as a page on the website. Update the public policies to match the actual features, vendors, and data practices in production.

This is an internal reminder, not legal advice.
